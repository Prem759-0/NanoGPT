# Moon AI — Sovereign Dataset Licensing & Provenance Policy

**Chief ML Systems Architect**: Prem Gaikwad (India 🇮🇳)  
**Policy Version**: `2.0.0`  
**Effective Date**: November 1, 2026  
**Ownership Mode**: STRICT SOVEREIGN — Zero Third-Party Pretrained Weights, Zero External Generative/Vision APIs.

---

## 1. Core Principles

Moon AI builds all foundational models from first principles using random weight initialization. To guarantee uncompromising legal safety, ethical sovereignty, and research reproducibility:
1. **Permissive Open Licenses Only**: Every media file must hold an explicit, verified open license permitting commercial AI training and derivative works.
2. **Zero Automated Vision-Language Labeling**: Pretrained third-party vision-language models (e.g. GPT-4V, Gemini Vision, BLIP, LLaVA) are strictly **prohibited** from generating or augmenting captions. Captions must be manually authored or provided directly by the media's original creators.
3. **Cryptographic Immutability**: Every asset is permanently bound to its SHA-256 checksum and perceptual difference hash (dHash).
4. **Verifiable Creator Attribution**: Anonymous, scraped, or unverified media without identifiable creators or source origins are strictly rejected.

---

## 2. Permissible Licenses Taxonomy

Only media bearing one of the following validated license identifiers are admitted into Moon AI manifests:

| License Identifier | Description | Commercial Training Allowed | Attribution Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CustomSovereign` | Original media created directly by Prem Gaikwad / Moon AI project | Yes | Yes (Project Attribution) |
| `CC0` | Creative Commons Zero (Public Domain Dedication) | Yes | Optional (Recommended) |
| `PublicDomain` | Assets in the worldwide public domain (expired copyright) | Yes | Optional |
| `CC-BY-4.0` | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International | Yes | **Mandatory** |
| `CC-BY-SA-4.0` | Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 | Yes | **Mandatory** |
| `MIT` | MIT Open Source License | Yes | **Mandatory** |
| `Apache-2.0` | Apache License 2.0 | Yes | **Mandatory** |
| `BSD-3-Clause` | 3-Clause BSD License | Yes | **Mandatory** |
| `OpenData` | Open Data Commons (ODbL / PDDL) | Yes | Varies |

---

## 3. Explicitly Prohibited Licenses & Sources

The following are strictly banned from all Moon AI dataset pipelines:
- ❌ **Non-Commercial Licenses (`CC-BY-NC`, `CC-BY-NC-SA`)**: Restricts broad deployment and sovereign commercial readiness.
- ❌ **No-Derivatives Licenses (`CC-BY-ND`)**: Forbids transformation and model weight representation.
- ❌ **Unlicensed Web Scraping**: Scraping Google Images, Pinterest, YouTube, Flickr, or stock websites without explicit permissive open licensing.
- ❌ **Synthetic Outputs from Proprietary AI Generators**: Images or videos generated by Midjourney, DALL-E 3, SDXL, Flux, Runway, Kling, or Sora cannot be included as genuine real-world source media.
- ❌ **Anonymous / Unknown Attribution**: Records with creator listed as `"Internet"`, `"Anonymous"`, `"None"`, or `"Unknown"` are automatically rejected by `validate_image_sources.py` and `validate_video_sources.py`.

2. **Automated Quality Gate**:
   [`datasets/tools/dataset_manager.py`](file:///d:/Downloads/linkden%20post/ai/datasets/tools/dataset_manager.py) performs audit passes over all manifests. Any record with an invalid or unverified license immediately marks `HYGIENE_VALID = False` and halts training pipelines.
